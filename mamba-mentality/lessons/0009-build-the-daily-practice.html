Two ideas, so far, have stayed theoretical. Today they become a specific thing you actually do.

You've now got two real tools: a mental-discipline mechanism (show up consistently, release the grip on outcome — abhyasa and vairagya, together) and a physiological reset (cyclic sighing, for when your body reacts before your mind does). Both are still, at this point, ideas you understand rather than things you do. This lesson closes that gap.

The structure itself is the point, not novelty: same time, same specific action, a spoken reminder that releases your grip on the outcome before you start. If your body spikes mid-session — frustration, tunnel vision, the urge to quit or force it — that's cyclic sighing's job: two inhales through the nose, one long exhale through the mouth, then continue.
