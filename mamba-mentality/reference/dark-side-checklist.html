The Dark Side Checklist

Ten distinctions between the trait and its corrosive twin — with the test for telling them apart, not just the definitions.

Built for Stage 8 (Lesson 24). Four rows are backed by specific cited research; three are this workspace's own reasoned distinctions, marked as such — don't mistake the second group for equally verified claims.
PatternHealthy sideDark sideThe test
Discipline / work ethic vs. compulsion / workaholism Researched Effort chosen because it's engaging Effort you can't choose to stop If you deliberately didn't do it today, would you feel okay, or real anxiety/guilt? (Schaufeli)
Confidence vs. ego Researched Stable, not contingent on the last result Fragile, needs constant validation How defensive are you when directly challenged? (Kernis)
High standards vs. perfectionism Researched Same standard, self-compassion when you miss it Same standard, harsh self-criticism when you miss it What's your inner voice's tone the moment you fall short? (Hewitt & Flett)
Competition vs. comparison Researched Extracts specific, usable information Collapses into a verdict on your worth When you notice someone better, what's the very next thought — a technique, or a feeling of being behind? (Festinger)
Ambition vs. insecurity Reasoned Moving toward a wanted future Fleeing a feared self-image Does the pursuit feel like approach, or escape?
Sacrifice vs. self-destruction Reasoned Preserves your capacity to keep building Depletes the capacity the goal itself needs Six months from now, will this "sacrifice" have left you more or less able to keep doing the work?
Persistence vs. stubbornness Reasoned Adjusts method, keeps the goal Refuses to adjust method despite clear evidence If this specific approach isn't working, can you change it without treating that as failure?
Pain tolerance vs. ignoring legitimate limits Case study A conscious, named risk, taken open-eyed Damage absorbed on autopilot, unexamined Could you say out loud, in the moment, exactly what you're risking and why? (Lesson 17)
Obsession with mastery vs. obsession with validation Core theme Would continue with zero audience Requires an audience to sustain Would you still do this if no one would ever find out? (Lesson 1)
