Manipulation Awareness

This is pattern-recognition for protecting yourself, never a diagnostic tool for labeling real people in your life. See MISSION.md's explicit scope note on this.

The Dark Triad

Three overlapping-but-distinct personality traits, named by Paulhus & Williams (2002): Narcissism (grandiosity, need for admiration, sensitivity to criticism), Machiavellianism (strategic manipulation, cynicism, self-interest), and Psychopathy (callousness, impulsivity, thrill-seeking). See Lesson 12 for the practically-important empathy nuance.

Pattern vs. Episode

The core discipline: judge people on repeated, cross-context patterns, never a single bad episode. A rough day is not evidence of character; a consistent, repeated shape across time and different situations is.

Social Engineering

Elicitation (feigned ignorance, flattery, deliberate false statements, manufactured rapport) and Pretexting (a fabricated identity or scenario) are Cialdini's persuasion principles aimed at extracting information instead of a sale. See Lesson 16. Defense: verify identity/urgency independently, never on the channel the requester initiated.
