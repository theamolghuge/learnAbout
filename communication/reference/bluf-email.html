BLUF Email Template

Bottom Line Up Front, in one page. From Lesson 2. Written twin of the Pyramid Principle.

The shape

Subject: [Decision/status in a few words] — [what you need, if anything]

Line 1 (BLUF): The answer, decision, or ask — one sentence.
Line 2: Action needed from the reader, and by when (or "no action needed, FYI").
Then: 2–3 supporting points, MECE, shortest first.
Last: Background/detail — only for readers who want it.

Subject line rule

The subject line should let the reader triage the email without opening it. A subject line is not a topic ("Project update") — it's an answer ("Project on track, no action needed") or an ask ("Need budget sign-off by Fri").

The two-line test

Quick test Cover everything after line 2. If a reader who only sees those two lines still knows what happened and what (if anything) they need to do, you've written BLUF correctly.

Common failure modes

  • Throat-clearing — "Hope you're doing well! Wanted to follow up on..." before the actual point. Cut it; the reader can tell you're being polite from the fact that you emailed at all.
  • Buried ask — the actual request is the last sentence, after four paragraphs of context.
  • No action line — reader finishes the email unsure whether they're supposed to do anything.
