- It gives an immediate, visible before/after (rambling vs. answer-first) within a 15-minute lesson.

Plan: alternate between Track 1/7 (thinking & delivery fundamentals) and the more applied tracks (2, 3, 4, 5, 6) so lessons interleave rather than exhaust one track before starting the next — consistent with the skill's interleaving principle for skills practice.
