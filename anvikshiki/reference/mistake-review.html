Mistake Review

Not for venting about what went wrong. For finding exactly where the reasoning broke.

Template

What happenedThe outcome, stated plainly — pratyaksha, not interpretation.
What I believed at the timeThe pratijna you were actually acting on, as precisely as you can reconstruct it.
Where it brokeRun it back through the five limbs (Lesson 5): was the hetu weak? Was udaharana never actually stated, or stated and not tested? Did upanaya get skipped? Name the specific step, not just "I was wrong."
Which hetvabhasa or biasIf one applies (Lessons 4, 10, 11, 15), name it specifically.
Was this avoidable with information I had?Distinguish a reasoning failure (the evidence was there, unchecked) from bad luck (the evidence genuinely wasn't available). Only the first is something to fix.
Is this part of a pattern?Check against recent weekly reviews — has this specific failure shown up before?
One specific changeNot "be more careful" — a specific step you'll add or check next time this situation recurs.
The point of this template A mistake review that ends at "I should have known better" hasn't located anything. One that ends at "I skipped udaharana's counter-example search, again, under time pressure" gives you something to actually watch for.
