Belief Journal

One entry per belief you actually examined, not one for everything you believe. From Lesson 20.

Template

Date 
PratijnaThe belief, stated plainly, one sentence, no hedging.

What belongs here

Not a diary of everything you thought today. An entry when a belief was actually run through the check — especially ones you'd been carrying unexamined, or ones a strong emotion was riding on (Lesson 9). The value is in the density of real checks over time, not in daily volume.
