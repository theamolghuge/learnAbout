The 90-Day Program

Twenty real lessons, paced across thirteen weeks — not ninety bespoke days. Padding this out to a unique unit per day would trade real content for the appearance of thoroughness.

Start the daily practice today, on day 1 — don't wait until you've reached Lesson 20. The lessons build the vocabulary; the practice runs the whole time underneath them.

This program assumes roughly 20-30 minutes on lesson days and 10-20 minutes on practice-only days, matching the ~30 min/day budget in MISSION.md. Adjust pacing rather than skip the practice days — the lessons build vocabulary, but the practice days are what actually build the habit.
