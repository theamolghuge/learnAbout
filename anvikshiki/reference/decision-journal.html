On review

Come back on the review date and fill in: what actually happened, which scenario it matched, and whether the reasoning that led here still looks sound in hindsight — not just whether the outcome was good. A good decision can have a bad outcome, and a bad decision can get lucky; the review is checking the reasoning, not just the score.
