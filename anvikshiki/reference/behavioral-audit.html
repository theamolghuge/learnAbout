Monthly Behavioral Audit

The monthly practice from Lesson 20, as a fillable checklist. Do this in one sitting, roughly 60-90 minutes.

1. Revealed values check

Run Lesson 7's calendar-and-wallet test on the past month specifically:

  • Where did discretionary time actually go, by category?
  • Where did discretionary money actually go?
  • Compare against your last stated set of priorities. Genuine match, or a gap? If a gap: false testimony, autopilot, or a real constraint (Lesson 7's three causes)?

2. Avoidance scan

Run Lesson 8's signals over the past month:

  • Any topic that produced dread, deflection, or chronic delay more than once?
  • Any belief you've been circling without ever running upanaya on it?

3. Glossary usage check

Skim the glossary. For each term, mark: used (reached for it in real reasoning this month) or theoretical (recognized it, didn't apply it). Pick two or three "theoretical" terms to deliberately practice next month.

4. Journal pattern check

Skim the past month's decision and belief journal entries and weekly reviews. Is the "one recurring pattern" from each week actually shrinking over time, or is it the same pattern every week? If it's the same pattern repeatedly, the weekly practice alone isn't enough — that's worth a dedicated mistake review, not just another note.

5. One sentence, written down

What's the single most useful thing this month's audit revealed? Write it as a sentence you could hand to next month's version of yourself.
