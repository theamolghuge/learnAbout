# Mission: Tech Interview Readiness

## Why
Prepare for technical interviews across two stacks — explain concepts, predict tricky output, and implement common patterns from memory.

## Success looks like
- Can explain any core topic with a short example
- Answer common interview questions confidently
- Understand what's happening underneath the framework, not just the annotations
