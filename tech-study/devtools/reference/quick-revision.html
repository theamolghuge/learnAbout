30-second answers

Merge vs rebase, one more time?
Merge preserves history + adds a merge commit. Rebase rewrites history into a line, new commit hashes.

Why is provided scope not packaged?
Assumes the runtime environment already supplies it (e.g. app-server-provided servlet API).

Why springdoc-openapi over hand-written docs?
Generated straight from the controllers — can't silently drift out of date.
